# TeleportMap

[![Use This Template badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/Use%20This%20Template-0?logo=github&labelColor=grey)][new-repo]


Are you tired of endlessly scrolling through the teleport menu?
Do you not like switching tabs, because you get lost quickly, and don't want to spend your time Looking for where your teleport Location is?
Do you wish you could "just have it all in front of you" and visually navigate to where you wanna go?

Then maybe this plugin is something for you.

### Activating in-game

To open the teleport map, just type /tpmap .
If you get stuck because the map is open and you can't type in Chat: Esc Always Closes the map.
